## Data dictionary
Universe: the 500 stocks that are constituents of a broad equity index at the end of month 120, with their monthly history back to month 1 or to the month they joined the index.

- `month`: Month index t (1 = first month of the sample).
- `stock_id`: Stock identifier.
- `book_to_market`: Book equity / market equity. Book equity from the latest annual report available at the end of month t; market equity at the end of month t.
- `gross_prof`: Gross profitability: gross profit / total assets, from the latest annual report available at the end of month t.
- `log_mktcap`: Natural log of market capitalization (USD millions) at the end of month t.
- `asset_growth`: Year-over-year growth in total assets, from the latest annual report available at the end of month t.
- `accruals`: Operating accruals / total assets, from the latest annual report available at the end of month t.
- `idio_vol`: Idiosyncratic volatility: annualized standard deviation of daily market-model residual returns over the 60 trading days ending at the end of month t.
- `leverage`: Total debt / total assets, from the latest annual report available at the end of month t.
- `turnover`: Average monthly share turnover (volume / shares outstanding) over the 3 months ending at the end of month t.
- `rd_intensity`: R&D expense / sales, from the latest annual report available at the end of month t.
- `ret_next`: Total stock return over month t+1 (end of month t to end of month t+1), in decimals. This is the prediction target.
