# Quality, Investment, and Low-Volatility Signals in a Monthly Equity Panel

## Abstract
Using 60,000 stock-month observations from 1,217 index constituents over 120 months, I evaluate nine point-in-time characteristics as predictors of next-month stock returns. Three signals survive statistical and economic screening: gross profitability is positive, while asset growth and idiosyncratic volatility are negative. The remaining six characteristics have weak rank ICs and long-short spreads that are statistically indistinguishable from zero. An equal-weighted composite of the three valid signals earns an estimated 0.96% per month before costs, or 11.6% annualized, with an approximate Sharpe ratio of 1.9. The results are consistent with quality, conservative-investment, and low-risk anomalies in this large-cap panel.

## Methods
The panel contains 500 stocks per month for 120 months, with point-in-time index membership and next-month total returns as the prediction target. For each feature, I use the reported average monthly Spearman rank IC with `ret_next`, its t-statistic, and the performance of an equal-weighted long-short quintile portfolio that buys the top quintile and sells the bottom quintile. I classify a feature as valid if its rank IC t-statistic exceeds 2 in absolute value and its long-short spread is economically meaningful. For negative signals, I reverse the long-short leg so that the strategy buys the bottom quintile and sells the top quintile. The composite strategy equally weights the direction-adjusted quintile spreads of all valid signals.

## Results
Three features are valid predictors:

| Feature | Direction | Mean rank IC | IC t-stat | Long-short monthly return | Long-short annualized Sharpe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `gross_prof` | Positive | 0.0378 | 6.07 | 1.16% | 1.94 |
| `asset_growth` | Negative | -0.0303 | -4.29 | -0.99% (reverse: +0.99%) | -1.48 (reverse: +1.48) |
| `idio_vol` | Negative | -0.0261 | -4.19 | -0.74% (reverse: +0.74%) | -1.20 (reverse: +1.20) |

The remaining six features are rejected. `book_to_market`, `log_mktcap`, `accruals`, `leverage`, `turnover`, and `rd_intensity` all have |t-stat| below 2 and long-short monthly returns no larger than 0.23% in absolute value. In particular, `book_to_market` has a near-zero mean rank IC of 0.0027, `log_mktcap` has a weak positive IC of 0.0055, and `accruals`, `leverage`, `turnover`, and `rd_intensity` have ICs close to zero with weak long-short performance.

A simple composite that equally weights the three valid signals—long high `gross_prof`, low `asset_growth`, and low `idio_vol`; short the opposite buckets—has an estimated monthly return of (1.16% + 0.99% + 0.74%) / 3 = 0.96%. This corresponds to an estimated gross annualized return of 11.6% before costs. Given the individual Sharpe ratios and likely positive but imperfect correlations among the signals, I estimate the composite annualized Sharpe ratio at approximately 1.9.

## Discussion
The strongest signal is gross profitability: firms with higher gross profit relative to assets earn reliably higher next-month returns. This is consistent with a quality/profitability premium. Asset growth is negative, meaning firms that expand their balance sheets rapidly subsequently underperform; this is consistent with investment-based and overvaluation explanations. Idiosyncratic volatility is also negative, supporting the low-risk anomaly: high-volatility stocks tend to deliver lower risk-adjusted returns.

The rejected signals are notable. Value, size, accruals, leverage, turnover, and R&D intensity show little predictive power in this large-cap index panel. This may reflect the sample’s focus on index constituents, where many classic anomalies are weaker, or may indicate that these characteristics are not robust in this particular 120-month period. The composite strategy is intentionally simple and transparent, but its performance is estimated before transaction costs and implementation frictions.

## Limitations
The universe is limited to 500 index constituents per month, so results may not generalize to microcaps or non-index stocks. Signal selection and performance are evaluated in-sample over 120 months, so the estimated premia may not persist out of sample. Expected performance is gross of transaction costs, shorting costs, borrow constraints, and market impact. Correlations among the three valid signals are not reported, so the composite Sharpe estimate is approximate. Equal-weighted quintile portfolios ignore sector, beta, and other factor exposures that could affect realized returns.
