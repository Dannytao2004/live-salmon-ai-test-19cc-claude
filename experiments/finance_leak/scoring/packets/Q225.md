# Title: Evaluating Equity Signals in a Monthly Stock Panel: The Danger of Look-Ahead Bias and Weak Anomalies

## Abstract
We analyze 10 firm characteristics for predicting next-month returns using a 120-month panel of 500 stocks per month. Only `analyst_rev` exhibits strong predictive power (mean rank IC = 0.296, long-short Sharpe = 26.4), but it is recorded at the end of month t+1 and is therefore unavailable at portfolio formation, indicating a clear look-ahead bias. After excluding it, `asset_growth` and `accruals` show marginally significant long-short quintile returns (t-stats ≈ 2.2) despite low rank ICs (≈ 0.006). The remaining features are statistically indistinguishable from noise. A combined long-short strategy on the two valid signals yields an expected annual return of about 3.4% with a Sharpe ratio of 0.7, though this result is fragile.

## Methods
We use a monthly panel of 500 index constituents over 120 months (60,000 observations). The target is `ret_next`, the total return over month t+1. All features except `analyst_rev` are known at the end of month t. `analyst_rev` is recorded at the end of month t+1, making it contemporaneous with the target and invalid for point-in-time prediction. We evaluate each feature using average monthly rank IC, its t-statistic, and an equal-weighted long-short quintile portfolio (buy top quintile, sell bottom quintile). A signal is considered valid if its long-short portfolio has a t-statistic greater than 2, while excluding variables with look-ahead bias.

## Results
`analyst_rev` is rejected due to look-ahead bias. Among the remaining features, only `asset_growth` and `accruals` have long-short t-statistics above 2. `asset_growth` has a mean rank IC of 0.0061, a long-short mean monthly return of 0.28%, and an annualized Sharpe of 0.72 (t ≈ 2.28). `accruals` has a mean rank IC of 0.0055, a long-short mean monthly return of 0.29%, and an annualized Sharpe of 0.70 (t ≈ 2.21). All other features have rank ICs between -0.0043 and 0.0043, t-statistics between -1.00 and 1.14, and long-short Sharpes between -0.27 and 0.26. An equal-weighted combination of the two valid signals yields an expected monthly return of about 0.285%, or 3.4% annualized, with an expected annualized Sharpe of approximately 0.70.

## Discussion
The dominant predictor in the panel is a look-ahead variable, underscoring the importance of strict point-in-time data discipline. After removing it, the remaining predictive power is weak and may be spurious due to multiple testing. The positive signs for `asset_growth` and `accruals` contradict well-documented anomalies (which predict negative returns for high growth and high accruals), suggesting these signals may not be robust out-of-sample. A strategy based on them would have modest expected performance and should be implemented with caution.

## Limitations
The sample covers only 120 months and 500 stocks per month, limiting statistical power. Equal-weighted portfolios ignore market capitalization and liquidity effects. Transaction costs, slippage, and short-selling constraints are not modeled. Multiple testing across 10 features increases the chance of false positives. The positive signs for `asset_growth` and `accruals` contradict established anomalies, suggesting potential spuriousness.
