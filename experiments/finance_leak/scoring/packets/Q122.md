# Timing-Aware Cross-Sectional Equity Signals in a Monthly Stock Panel

## Abstract
We analyze a 60,000-row monthly stock panel of 1,217 stocks and 10 candidate characteristics. Three signals are both economically motivated and statistically robust after checking information timing: gross profitability is positively predictive, while asset growth and idiosyncratic volatility are negatively predictive. Analyst revisions show an extreme apparent rank IC of 0.296, but this feature is recorded at the end of month t+1, the same period as the target return, so it is invalid for real-time prediction and would induce look-ahead bias. A monthly long-short composite of the three valid signals has an estimated gross annual return of about 11.6% and an expected annualized Sharpe near 1.8 before costs. The remaining characteristics are statistically weak in this sample and are not used in the strategy.

## Methods
The dataset contains one row per stock per month for months 1–120, with 500 index constituents each month. The target is `ret_next`, the stock return over month t+1. For a feature to be valid, it must be observable at the end of month t, before the return period begins. I also require a plausible economic mechanism, a rank IC t-stat greater than 2 in absolute value, and consistency in the sign of the long-short quintile return.

Signal strength is measured by average monthly cross-sectional Spearman rank IC and by the mean monthly return of an equal-weighted long-short portfolio that buys the top quintile and sells the bottom quintile of the feature. For the strategy, I combine valid signals by equal-weighted ranks. Negative predictors are reversed so that higher composite ranks always correspond to higher expected returns. The composite portfolio is long the top quintile and short the bottom quintile, rebalanced monthly.

Timing is critical. `book_to_market`, `gross_prof`, `log_mktcap`, `asset_growth`, `accruals`, `leverage`, and `rd_intensity` are based on information available at the end of month t. `idio_vol` uses daily returns through the end of month t, and `turnover` uses the three months ending at month t. `analyst_rev`, however, is recorded at the end of month t+1, the same month as `ret_next`; it is therefore not tradable at the end of month t.

## Results
The valid tradable signals are shown below.

| Feature | Direction | Mean rank IC | IC t-stat | Long-short mean monthly return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `gross_prof` | Positive | 0.0378 | 6.07 | 1.16% |
| `asset_growth` | Negative | -0.0303 | -4.29 | -0.99% |
| `idio_vol` | Negative | -0.0261 | -4.19 | -0.74% |

`gross_prof` is the strongest tradable signal: profitable firms outperform unprofitable firms. `asset_growth` is negative: firms with high year-over-year asset growth underperform, consistent with the investment anomaly. `idio_vol` is negative: high idiosyncratic-volatility stocks underperform lower-volatility stocks within this index universe.

Several other features have weak or unreliable evidence. `book_to_market` has the expected positive sign but a rank IC of only 0.0027 with t-stat 0.68. `log_mktcap` is positive but weak, with IC 0.0055 and t-stat 1.26. `accruals` has IC 0.0015 and t-stat 0.36. `leverage`, `turnover`, and `rd_intensity` all have t-stats below 0.5 in absolute value. These are rejected for this strategy because they are statistically indistinguishable from noise in this sample.

`analyst_rev` presents the most important methodological issue. Its rank IC is 0.2963, its t-stat is 80.29, and its long-short portfolio averages 8.73% per month with an annualized Sharpe of 24.23. This is not a tradable anomaly in the given setup: the feature is recorded at the end of month t+1, the same period as `ret_next`. Using it at the end of month t would be look-ahead bias.

For the composite strategy, I equal-weight the three valid signals after reversing `asset_growth` and `idio_vol`. The average exploitable monthly long-short return is approximately:

\[
(1.16\% + 0.99\% + 0.74\%)/3 \approx 0.96\%.
\]

This implies roughly 11.6% gross annual arithmetic return, or about 12.2% compounded. Assuming moderate positive correlation among the signals, the combined Sharpe is likely in the 1.7–1.9 range, well above each signal alone but below the unrealistic Sharpe implied by `analyst_rev`.

## Discussion
The three valid signals capture distinct and well-known equity effects. Gross profitability is a quality signal: firms with high gross profit relative to assets tend to be more profitable and are rewarded in the cross-section. Asset growth is an investment signal: aggressive asset expansion often predicts lower future returns, consistent with overinvestment or empire-building behavior. Idiosyncratic volatility captures the low-volatility anomaly: high-volatility stocks are often overpriced or subject to lottery-demand effects, leading to lower risk-adjusted returns.

A composite of these signals is attractive because the underlying economic stories are different. Profitability, investment, and volatility should not be perfectly correlated, so combining them can improve the information ratio relative to any single signal. The strategy is a standard monthly long-short quintile portfolio, which is simple to implement and interpret.

The rejected signals are not necessarily economically irrelevant in all settings. Value, accruals, and leverage have classic motivations, but in this particular index-constituent panel and sample period they do not show reliable cross-sectional predictive power. `analyst_rev` would be very powerful if it were available at the formation date, but as specified it is contemporaneous with the target return and therefore cannot be used without look-ahead bias. A realistic implementation would require either lagging analyst revisions or forecasting them from earlier data.

## Limitations
- The panel contains only 500 index constituents per month, so results may not generalize to microcaps or the full cross-section of stocks.
- Transaction costs, borrow fees, market impact, and shorting constraints are not modeled; net returns would be lower than the estimated gross returns.
- The composite uses equal weights rather than an out-of-sample optimized weighting scheme.
- The 120-month sample is modest, and the rejected signals may be weak rather than entirely absent.
- `analyst_rev` is contemporaneous with `ret_next` in this dataset and cannot be traded as specified without look-ahead bias.
