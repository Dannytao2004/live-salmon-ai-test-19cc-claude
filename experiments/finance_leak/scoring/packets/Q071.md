# Predicting Next-Month Stock Returns from Firm Characteristics

## Abstract
We evaluate nine firm characteristics as cross-sectional predictors of next-month stock returns in a panel of up to 500 index constituents over 120 months. Using monthly rank information coefficients and quintile long-short portfolios, we find that only leverage is a statistically reliable signal, with a mean rank IC of 0.0164 (t-stat = 2.74) and a top-minus-bottom quintile annualized Sharpe ratio of 0.92. High-leverage stocks tend to outperform low-leverage stocks. The other eight characteristics are not statistically distinguishable from zero after our validity screen. A monthly-rebalanced long-short leverage strategy is estimated to earn about 6.2% per year gross of costs.

## Methods
The dataset consists of 34,708 stock-month observations from 120 months. For each feature, we compute the cross-sectional Spearman rank correlation with next-month returns within each month, then average across months to obtain the mean rank IC. The t-statistic is computed as the mean rank IC divided by its time-series standard deviation, scaled by the square root of the number of months. We also form equal-weighted quintile portfolios each month and report the return of the top quintile minus the bottom quintile. A feature is treated as a valid signal if its rank IC t-statistic exceeds 2.0 in absolute value and its long-short return direction is consistent with the IC sign.

## Results
The table below summarizes the predictive results for all nine features.

| Feature | Mean rank IC | IC t-stat | L/S monthly return | Annualized Sharpe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| leverage | 0.0164 | 2.74 | 0.52% | 0.92 |
| asset_growth | 0.0055 | 1.16 | 0.37% | 0.74 |
| accruals | 0.0059 | 1.03 | 0.24% | 0.41 |
| idio_vol | 0.0056 | 0.86 | 0.13% | 0.19 |
| log_mktcap | 0.0014 | 0.26 | -0.07% | -0.14 |
| gross_prof | 0.0012 | 0.23 | 0.12% | 0.22 |
| rd_intensity | 0.0010 | 0.20 | -0.06% | -0.11 |
| turnover | -0.0005 | -0.10 | 0.13% | 0.23 |
| book_to_market | -0.0017 | -0.28 | -0.05% | -0.08 |

Leverage is the only feature that passes the pre-specified validity threshold. Its mean monthly rank IC is 0.0164, with an IC t-statistic of 2.74 and positive IC in 59% of months. The equal-weighted top-minus-bottom quintile leverage portfolio earns 0.52% per month, implying an annualized gross return of approximately 6.2% and an annualized Sharpe ratio of 0.92. Although asset_growth and accruals show positive long-short returns, their rank ICs are not statistically significant at conventional levels.

## Discussion
The positive predictive power of leverage suggests that high-leverage firms have earned higher subsequent returns than low-leverage firms in this sample. This is broadly consistent with a risk-based interpretation: more levered firms may be more exposed to distress or credit risk, for which investors demand compensation. However, the effect may also be specific to this index-survivor sample and this particular period. The other characteristics, including value, profitability, and idiosyncratic volatility, do not exhibit reliable standalone predictive power here.

## Limitations
- The sample is constructed from stocks that are index constituents at the end of month 120, which introduces survivorship and selection bias.
- The reported strategy performance is gross of transaction costs, borrowing costs, and short-selling constraints.
- The leverage signal is significant at the single-feature 5% level but would not survive a strict Bonferroni adjustment for testing nine features.
- Fundamental signals rely on annual report data assumed to be available at month-end; reporting delays or restatements could affect implementability.
