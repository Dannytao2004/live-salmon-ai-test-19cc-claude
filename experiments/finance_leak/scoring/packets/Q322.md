# Standardized Unexpected Earnings and Cross-Sectional Equity Returns: Evidence from a 120-Month Panel

## Abstract
We evaluate ten stock characteristics as monthly cross-sectional predictors of next-month returns using a panel of 500 index constituents over 120 months. Among all features, only standardized unexpected earnings (SUE) is a robust and economically large signal: its mean monthly rank IC is 0.0998 with a t-statistic of 23.25, and a top-minus-bottom quintile spread earns 3.04% per month. All other characteristics have rank IC t-statistics below 1.7 and negligible long-short Sharpe ratios. A monthly rebalanced SUE quintile spread is proposed, with an expected gross annual return of approximately 36.5% and an annualized Sharpe ratio of 7.31.

## Methods
The dataset is a point-in-time monthly panel with 120 months and 500 stocks per month. For each feature, we measure cross-sectional predictive power using the Spearman rank correlation between the feature and next-month return, averaged across months. The t-statistic is computed as the mean rank IC divided by its standard deviation times the square root of the number of months. We also form equal-weighted quintile portfolios each month and compute the return spread between the top and bottom quintiles. A feature is treated as a valid signal if its rank IC t-statistic exceeds conventional significance thresholds and its long-short portfolio has clear economic magnitude.

## Results
SUE is the only statistically significant and economically meaningful signal. It has a mean monthly rank IC of 0.0998, an IC standard deviation of 0.0470, and a t-statistic of 23.25. The sign of the IC is positive in 99% of months. The equal-weighted top-quintile-minus-bottom-quintile SUE portfolio earns 3.04% per month, with an annualized Sharpe ratio of 7.31.

The other nine features do not pass the significance threshold. Book-to-market has a mean rank IC of -0.0030 with a t-statistic of -0.72. Gross profitability has a rank IC of 0.0039 with a t-statistic of 0.93. Log market cap has a rank IC of 0.0001 with a t-statistic of 0.02. Asset growth has a rank IC of 0.0061 with a t-statistic of 1.64. Accruals has a rank IC of 0.0055 with a t-statistic of 1.34. Idiosyncratic volatility has a rank IC of 0.0043 with a t-statistic of 1.14. Leverage has a rank IC of -0.0043 with a t-statistic of -1.00. Turnover has a rank IC of 0.0001 with a t-statistic of 0.02. R&D intensity has a rank IC of -0.0002 with a t-statistic of -0.05.

## Discussion
The strong positive relation between SUE and next-month returns is consistent with post-earnings announcement drift: firms with large positive earnings surprises continue to outperform, while firms with large negative surprises continue to underperform. The proposed strategy exploits this by buying the top quintile of SUE and selling the bottom quintile each month. At gross returns, the strategy has an expected monthly return of 3.04%, implying an arithmetic annualized return of about 36.5% and an annualized Sharpe ratio of 7.31. Other valuation, profitability, size, investment, accrual, volatility, leverage, turnover, and R&D signals do not appear reliable in this panel.

## Limitations
The reported performance is gross of transaction costs, borrowing costs, and short-sale constraints. The sample is limited to 120 months and 500 index constituents per month, so results may not generalize. No adjustment is made for multiple testing or for common systematic risk factors. The SUE signal depends on timely and accurate consensus earnings forecast data, which may be revised or unavailable for some stocks.
