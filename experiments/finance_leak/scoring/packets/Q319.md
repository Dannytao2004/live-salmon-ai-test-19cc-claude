# Cross-Sectional Stock Return Predictors: Evidence from a Monthly Panel

## Abstract
We evaluate nine stock characteristics as predictors of next-month returns in a panel of 500 index constituents over 120 months. Using monthly cross-sectional Spearman rank correlations, only leverage has a statistically reliable positive signal, with a mean rank IC of 0.0164 (t-stat = 2.74) and a top-minus-bottom quintile return of 0.52% per month, corresponding to an annualized Sharpe of 0.92. The other eight characteristics, including book-to-market and gross profitability, have economically small and statistically insignificant rank ICs. A monthly long-short leverage quintile strategy is estimated to earn about 6.2% per year before transaction costs.

## Methods
For each month and each feature, we compute the cross-sectional Spearman rank correlation between the feature and next-month returns (`ret_next`). We average these rank ICs over the 120 months and compute t-statistics as the mean rank IC divided by its standard deviation times the square root of the number of months. We also form equal-weighted long-short portfolios that buy the top quintile and sell the bottom quintile of each feature, rebalanced monthly. A feature is treated as a valid signal if its rank IC t-statistic exceeds 2.0 in absolute value and its long-short return has the same sign as the rank IC.

## Results
| Feature | Mean rank IC | IC t-stat | Long-short monthly return | Long-short annualized Sharpe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| book_to_market | -0.0017 | -0.28 | -0.05% | -0.08 |
| gross_prof | 0.0012 | 0.23 | 0.12% | 0.22 |
| log_mktcap | 0.0014 | 0.26 | -0.07% | -0.14 |
| asset_growth | 0.0055 | 1.16 | 0.37% | 0.74 |
| accruals | 0.0059 | 1.03 | 0.24% | 0.41 |
| idio_vol | 0.0056 | 0.86 | 0.13% | 0.19 |
| leverage | 0.0164 | 2.74 | 0.52% | 0.92 |
| turnover | -0.0005 | -0.10 | 0.13% | 0.23 |
| rd_intensity | 0.0010 | 0.20 | -0.06% | -0.11 |

Only leverage meets the statistical significance threshold. Its positive mean rank IC of 0.0164 is supported by a positive long-short quintile return of 0.52% per month and a positive IC in 59% of months. Asset growth and accruals show positive but statistically weak evidence, with t-statistics of 1.16 and 1.03, respectively. The remaining features have rank ICs near zero and inconsistent long-short return signs.

## Discussion
The positive relation between leverage and next-month returns suggests that higher-leverage stocks earn higher expected returns in this sample, consistent with a risk-based or distress-risk interpretation. However, the signal is modest: a monthly rank IC of 0.0164 is economically small, though the top-minus-bottom quintile spread reaches about 6.2% annualized. Several well-known predictors such as book-to-market and gross profitability do not show significant predictive power here, possibly because of the sample period, index-universe construction, or the use of raw annual accounting data without further adjustments.

## Limitations
- The sample covers only 120 months and one equity universe, so the results may be period-specific.
- Transaction costs, short-selling costs, and liquidity constraints are not considered.
- The universe consists of current index constituents with history backfilled, which may introduce survivorship or selection bias.
- Multiple-testing adjustments would raise the significance threshold; leverage remains the best candidate but is not robust to the strictest corrections.
